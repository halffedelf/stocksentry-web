Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days of full access to the Pro tier. We do not require a credit card upfront. When your trial ends, the bot will message you with a link to subscribe via Stripe to keep your watchlist active.

How are payments processed?

All payments are securely handled by Stripe. StockSentry never sees or stores your payment information. You will receive a unique checkout link directly inside your Telegram chat.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel anytime by typing /account in the bot. It will provide a link to your Stripe customer portal where you can cancel your subscription with one click. Your access will continue until the end of your billing cycle.

Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Yes. You can manage your tier from your Stripe portal at any time. Pricing adjustments are prorated automatically.
