Privacy Policy

Zero financial visibility. Period.

The Strict Boundaries of StockSentry

StockSentry operates on a principle of absolute minimum data collection. Because we do not act as your financial advisor, we structurally ensure we have no insight into your personal finances.

What We Collect (Only 2 Things):

  • Your Telegram ID: The numerical string required by the Telegram API to route messages back to your chat.
  • Your Inputted Tickers & Entry Prices: The data you actively send to the bot via /watch or /bought commands to trigger monitoring.

What We DO NOT Collect:

  • We cannot see your brokerage account, login credentials, or transaction history.
  • We do not know your real name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
  • We do not know your portfolio size, income, net worth, or financial status.

Third-Party Subprocessors

To deliver the service, we interact with the following third parties. We only share the absolute minimum data required to execute their specific function.

  • Stripe (Payment Processing) When you subscribe, you are redirected to Stripe. StockSentry does not store or process your credit card information. Stripe maintains your billing details.
  • Telegram (Messaging) All communication occurs over Telegram's infrastructure. We are subject to Telegram's privacy policy regarding message transit.
  • Anthropic / Claude (AI Analysis) When an alert is generated, we send the technical financial data to Claude to write the plain-English summary. Your Telegram ID is never sent to the AI. The AI only sees raw market data (e.g., "AAPL RSI is 25, write a summary").

Data Deletion

If you cancel your subscription and block the bot on Telegram, you can request a complete wipe of your tracked tickers and historical agent interactions by contacting support.
