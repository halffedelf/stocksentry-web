Step 02 · Discover

Find high-confidence setups.

The Sentry doesn't just find stocks — it evaluates them. Every afternoon it sweeps the S&P 100 (and NASDAQ 100 on Premium) through a 3-prong rubric and surfaces only the high-confidence candidates.

Confidence engine — 0–100% rubric score, technical + fundamental + macro.
Sentiment catalyst — fresh news weighted into every verdict.
No noise — only high-confidence picks reach your chat.
Step 03 · Watch

Build your watchlist.

Tell the agent which tickers to monitor. One command — the Sentry takes it from there, scanning the chart and the news for the right entry moment.

Step 04 · Log

Log your positions.

When you enter a trade, tell the Sentry your exact fill price. That lets it calculate stop-loss levels and R/R tailored to your entry — not a generic average.

Step 05 · Monitor

Delegate the monitoring.

The Sentry re-checks every position every 30 minutes against 14 technical indicators and live news sentiment. You get a ping only when it's time to act.
