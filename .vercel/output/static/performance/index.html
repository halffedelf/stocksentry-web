Backtest assumptions FAQ

The honest questions.

Backtests lie when the assumptions hide. Here's what ours assumed.

Is this real money, or simulation?

Simulation, with the same rubric the live bot uses today. The Sentry does not execute trades for you — it generates the signal; you place the order with your own broker. This page reports what the signal stream would have produced under the assumptions listed below.

Did you tune the model on this period?

No. The rubric was frozen in mid-2024, before the audit was executed. No parameter touched the 2021–2024 window during tuning. Out-of-sample evaluation by construction.

Slippage and fills — realistic?

We use 0.05% per-leg slippage on every entry and exit, applied to the next-day open after a signal. For S&P 100 names this is conservative on average; for smaller-cap names it would be optimistic — which is why the universe is restricted to S&P 100.

What's missing from this number?

Taxes (you pay short-term capital gains on positions held < 1 year), broker spread differences, and the emotional discipline cost of actually placing every order the bot recommends. Live results vary with your execution.

Will the next four years look the same?

No. Past performance is not predictive of future results — that's not a disclaimer, it's a fact. The audit covered a bull, a bear, and a recovery. Whether the next regime is similar is unknowable; the system's job is to adapt to it the same way it adapted to the last one.

Can I see every trade?

Yes. The full CSV of all 64 trades — entry date, exit date, ticker, prices, P&L, exit reason — is available by request. Email alpha@stocksentry.bot.
