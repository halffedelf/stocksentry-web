Frequently Asked Questions

Operations & Security

Does StockSentry execute trades for me?

  • No. StockSentry is an intelligence and alerting tool only.
  • We identify setups and send Telegram notifications.
  • You are responsible for executing trades manually in your own brokerage account.

Do you connect to my brokerage account?

  • No. We operate with zero financial visibility.
  • We do not ask for API keys, login credentials, or account balances.
  • We only store your Telegram ID and the specific ticker symbols you ask the bot to watch.

Why Telegram instead of a standalone app?

  • Speed: Telegram's API allows for near-instant push notification delivery.
  • Efficiency: No heavy charting app running in your background draining battery.
  • Frictionless: You likely already have the app open; no context switching required to get an alert.

What markets do you cover?

  • Currently limited to US Equities.
  • Discovery scans are restricted to the highly liquid S&P 100 and NASDAQ 100 components.
  • OTC, crypto, and forex are not supported.

Billing & Account

How does the 14-day free trial work?

  • You receive 14 days of unrestricted access to the Pro tier features.
  • No credit card is required to initiate the trial.
  • After 14 days, the bot will cease monitoring unless you initiate a paid subscription via Stripe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

  • Send the command /account to the bot in Telegram.
  • The bot will return a secure, temporary link to your Stripe Customer Portal.
  • Click "Cancel Plan". Service remains active until the end of your billing cycle.

Do you offer refunds?

  • No. Due to the nature of data processing costs, we do not offer prorated refunds for mid-cycle cancellations.
  • This is why we provide a strict, no-credit-card-required 14-day trial for evaluation.

Is this financial advice?

  • Absolutely not. StockSentry is an algorithmic data tool.
  • Alerts are generated based on mathematical technical indicators, not personalized fiduciary assessment.
  • All trading involves risk. You are solely responsible for your financial decisions.

Usage & Strategy

How can I find new tickers to watch or buy?

  • Rely on the automated discovery scans.
  • The agent sweeps the S&P 100 (and NASDAQ 100 on higher tiers) at the end of the trading day.
  • It surfaces high-probability technical setups that are not currently on your watchlist.

Does the app track after-market price changes?

  • No. Active monitoring occurs strictly during standard market hours.
  • If a stock's trend changes drastically overnight, it will be captured and reported in your 8:30 AM ET morning briefing.

Is it worth paying for this app?

  • Yes, provided you use it correctly.
  • Continuous monitoring helps limit your losses and lock in gains by catching trend reversals you might miss.
  • The agent reports your P&L daily, so you can measure its value yourself.
  • The Catch: The math only works if you are honest with the bot. You must accurately log your exact entry prices via the /bought command for alerts to trigger accurately.

How can I make the best use of the app?

  • Log holdings accurately: Add owned tickers and exact entry prices via /bought.
  • Check scans: Review discovery scans (Daily for Pro/Premium; Mon, Wed, Thu for Basic).
  • Build watchlist: Use /watch on promising scan results so the agent tracks them every 30 minutes.
  • Execute & Update: Wait for a buy alert, do your due diligence, execute the trade, and immediately log it as /bought so the agent can monitor.

Will the agent get better with time?

  • Yes. The agent evaluates its own performance.
  • Every call (buy, sell, set stop-loss) is recorded in the database.
  • The system tracks the ticker's trend for up to 30 days to verify if the call was correct, feeding this data back into the analysis loop to calibrate future signals.

How exactly is AI involved?

  • The agent pulls historical price data, 14 technical indicators, and real-time news sentiment.
  • The AI layer processes these diverse data points against strict trading rules.
  • It calculates a definitive verdict and translates complex chart setups into plain English.
