How StockSentry Works

You tell it what you own and what you're watching. It monitors the markets on your behalf and messages you when something needs your attention.

1

Add your tickers

Getting started takes under a minute. Send StockSentry a command in Telegram:

/bought AAPL 190.50 Log a stock you own at the price you paid.
/watch NVDA Add a stock to your watchlist without committing.

Every ticker is validated against Yahoo Finance before being saved. You can add, update, or remove tickers any time with /sold.

2

Monitors every 30 minutes

During market hours, StockSentry checks every ticker in your portfolio and watchlist every 30 minutes. It pulls live price data, volume, and calculates 14 technical indicators across two categories, alongside real-time news sentiment.

Sell / Risk Signals

For owned stocks

  • Stop-loss trigger
  • Support level breach
  • RSI overbought
  • Death Cross (50/200-day MA)
  • Bearish MACD crossover
  • Bollinger Band upper breach

Buy / Entry Signals

For tracked stocks

  • Breakout above resistance
  • RSI oversold
  • Golden Cross
  • Volume spike
  • Bullish MACD crossover
  • Bollinger Band lower touch
  • Low P/E ratio & High dividend yield
3

You only hear when it matters

StockSentry stays silent unless something has changed. When a signal fires, it asks an AI layer for a verdict and only sends you a message if the AI agrees the signal is actionable. What you receive is plain English, not a chart.

⚠️ SET STOP-LOSS — META
$512.40 | 🟢 +8.3% from entry
Set stop at: $490.00
If triggered, sell immediately.
Why: Death cross forming, volume declining, approaching resistance.
💡 BUY OPPORTUNITY — NVDA
$118.20 | RSI 31.4
Allocate: 5% of portfolio
Stop-loss: $112.00 | Risk/reward: 2.8:1
Why: RSI oversold, bullish MACD crossover, high volume on bounce from support.
4

Morning briefing, every trading day

At 8:00 AM ET Monday–Friday, StockSentry sends you a personalised portfolio briefing covering:

  • Current price and P&L for every owned stock.
  • Estimated resistance targets & suggested stop-loss levels.
  • Portfolio-wide average P&L (7-day and 30-day trends).
  • Sector concentration warnings if overweight.
5

Discovery Scan

Every evening, StockSentry screens the S&P 100 (and NASDAQ 100 on higher tiers) for strong technical setups you aren't tracking.

Run manually anytime with /scan. Add surfaced tickers with /watch to begin 30-minute monitoring.

6

Price Alerts

Set specific price thresholds on any valid ticker (owned or unowned) to receive an immediate ping.

/alert AAPL 220 // Alert if > $220
/alert AAPL 180 below // Alert if < $180
7

Self-tracking performance

Every recommendation made is recorded with the price at the time of the call. Each morning, StockSentry checks whether those calls played out correctly to build a transparent track record (win rate, average outcome, top signal types).

This data feeds back into the AI layer. The longer the agent runs, the more calibrated its signaling becomes.

StockSentry analyses data. You make the decisions.
What StockSentry doesn't do

It is not a financial adviser. Every alert is based on publicly available market data and technical signals. StockSentry has no visibility into your brokerage account, portfolio size, income, or financial situation.

It cannot and does not provide personalised investment advice. Every message StockSentry sends is a data point for your own research, not an instruction to trade.
