How StockSentry Works

You tell it what you own and what you're watching. It monitors the markets and messages you when action is required.

1

Connect via Telegram

StockSentry operates entirely within the Telegram messaging app. You do not need to download a separate charting application.

  • Install Telegram Download the app for iOS, Android, or Desktop.
  • Create an Account Register with your phone number. Note: StockSentry cannot see your phone number; it only registers your anonymous Telegram ID.
  • Start the Bot Open t.me/StockSentryAYS_bot in your browser, allow it to open Telegram, and tap START at the bottom of the chat to begin your 14-day trial.
2

Add your tickers

Initial setup takes seconds. Send StockSentry a command directly in the chat to start monitoring.

  • /bought AAPL 190.50
    Log a stock you own at the exact price you paid.
  • /watch NVDA
    Add a stock to your watchlist for entry monitoring.

Every ticker is validated against Yahoo Finance before being saved. You can add, update, or remove tickers any time using /sold.

3

Monitors every 30 minutes

During market hours, StockSentry checks every ticker in your portfolio and watchlist every 30 minutes. It pulls live price data, volume, and calculates 14 technical indicators across two categories, alongside real-time news sentiment.

Risk / Exit Signals

For owned stocks

  • Stop-loss target hit
  • Support level breach
  • RSI overbought
  • Death Cross (50/200-day MA)
  • Bearish MACD crossover
  • Bollinger Band upper breach

Entry Signals

For tracked stocks

  • Breakout above resistance
  • RSI oversold
  • Golden Cross
  • Volume spike
  • Bullish MACD crossover
  • Bollinger Band lower touch
4

You only hear when it matters

StockSentry stays silent unless something has changed. When a signal fires, the AI layer assesses the setup and alerts you if it deems the data actionable.

What you receive is a plain-English briefing detailing exactly why the alert triggered, the suggested stop-loss, and the current market context—not a chart you have to interpret yourself.

Morning briefing, every trading day

At 8:00 AM ET Monday–Friday, StockSentry sends you a personalised portfolio briefing detailing exactly where your assets stand before the bell rings.

  • Current price and P&L for every owned stock against your entry price.
  • Estimated resistance targets & calculated stop-loss levels.
  • Portfolio-wide average P&L history over 7 and 30 day trends.
  • Sector concentration warnings if you become heavily overweight in tech/growth.

Discovery Scan

Every evening, StockSentry screens the S&P 100 (and the NASDAQ 100 on higher tiers) for strong technical setups you aren't currently tracking.

You receive heavily vetted buy opportunities complete with allocation sizing, calculated risk/reward ratios, and links to pertinent financial news driving the momentum.

7

Price Alerts

Set specific price thresholds on any valid ticker (whether you own it or not) to receive an immediate ping when the price level is crossed.

/alert AAPL 220 // Alert if > $220
/alert AAPL 180 below // Alert if < $180
8

Self-tracking performance

Every alert is recorded. Each morning, StockSentry checks whether its recent setup analyses played out correctly to build a transparent track record.

This data feeds back into the AI layer. The longer the agent runs, the more calibrated its signaling becomes.

Under the hood

System Implementation
Data source Yahoo Finance (live prices, fundamentals)
Technical signals 14 indicators across price action, momentum, and volume
Analysis layer Claude AI (Anthropic) — verdict & plain-English reasoning
Monitoring freq. Every 30 minutes during US market hours

Storage Ticker list and Telegram ID only — no personal data
Performance Every setup analysis recorded and evaluated daily
StockSentry analyses data. You make the decisions.
What StockSentry doesn't do

It is not a financial adviser. Every alert is based on publicly available market data and technical signals. StockSentry has no visibility into your brokerage account, portfolio size, income, or financial situation.

It cannot and does not provide personalised investment advice. Every message StockSentry sends is a data point for your own research, not an instruction to trade.
